# Copywriting Rules (Social Media)

## Anti-Marketese

| ❌ Never | ✅ Instead |
|---|---|
| "hottest," "world-class," "revolutionary," "game-changing" | Facts and numbers carry the claim |
| "You need this!" "Don't miss out!" | State the fact. No drama |
| Em dashes (—) | Commas, colons, or separate sentences |
| Hustle-culture or corporate buzzwords ("grind," "synergy," "leverage," "scale," "game-changer") | Plain, direct language |
| Long unbroken sentences | Varied rhythm. Mix short and medium. Avoid a run of short punchy sentences that sound like a sales script |

## The "Not-X-But-Y" Ban

**CRITICAL: Do not use false contrarian or "Not-X-But-Y" reframing hooks.** This is the single most common AI copywriting tell.

Examples of what to **AVOID:**
- "Most businesses don't have a traffic problem. They have a conversion problem."
- "You don't need more time. You need more focus."
- "It's not about working harder. It's about working smarter."
- "The problem isn't your price. It's your positioning."

**Why:** Feels formulaic, robotic, and reads like generic 2010s internet marketing copy. Audiences recognize the pattern and tune out.

**Instead:** State the core insight directly, or use a narrative, conversational opening without the artificial contrast.

| ❌ Not-X-But-Y | ✅ Direct |
|---|---|
| "Most businesses don't have a traffic problem. They have a conversion problem." | "Your website gets visitors. They leave without calling. That's a conversion problem. Here's how to spot it." |
| "You don't need more leads. You need better follow-up." | "You're getting leads. They're going cold before the second touchpoint. Let's fix that." |

## Language Rules

1. **Use contractions.** "You're" not "you are." "It's" not "it is." "Don't" not "do not." Natural speech, not formal writing.
2. **Simple words over complex.** "Use" not "utilize." "Fix" not "remediate." "Show" not "demonstrate." The goal is maximum ease of understanding on the first read.
3. **Varied sentence rhythm.** Avoid three or more short sentences in a row. Mix lengths. A short statement. Then a slightly longer one that explains or elaborates. Then maybe a medium one that bridges to the next point.
4. **No dramatic phrasing.** "The hard truth," "Here's the brutal reality," "Wake up call" — all signal AI-generated marketing copy. Just state the information.

## Scannability (Mobile-First)

1. **Half word count.** Cut every caption by 50% after drafting. Then cut again.
2. **One idea per line.** New line = new thought. No paragraph stacking.
3. **Inverted pyramid.** Hook = the conclusion. Body = supporting evidence. CTA = next step.
4. **Visual anchors.** Bullet points, bold keywords, emoji as section markers. Not decoration: navigation.

## Four Readers, One Caption

| Reader | Behavior | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Skimmer** (3-5s) | Reads headline only | Headline alone must deliver the promise. If it needs the subhead to make sense, it's too vague. |
| **Data Hunter** (scans) | Looks for numbers/proof | Stats close to claims. Visually distinct (bold, bullets). |
| **Selective Reader** | Reads headline + subheads | Subheads bridge claim → delivery. Don't use subheads to rescue a weak headline. |
| **Detail Reader** | Reads everything | No gaps. Logic holds up under scrutiny. |

## Caption Structure Template

```
[Hook — 1-2 lines. Direct, conversational, no artificial contrast.]
[Blank line]
[Supporting point — mix of sentence lengths]
[Blank line]
[Data point or stat — bolded, visually anchored to the claim above]

[THEN CHOOSE ONE CLOSING BELOW. GENERATE BOTH, PRESENT BOTH.]

--- CTA CLOSING ---
[One line. Next action. Use a contraction. Must be natural, not pushy.]
[Blank line]
[Hashtags — 3-5 max]

--- NON-CTA CLOSING ---
[One line. Conversational wrap. No link, no button, no "learn more." Leave the reader with the idea, not the pitch.]
[Blank line]
[Hashtags — 3-5 max]
```

## CTA vs Non-CTA Rules

Many posts should deliver free value without asking for anything. Others should invite a next step. Generate both closings and let the human decide.

| CTA Closing | Non-CTA Closing |
|---|---|
| Natural invitation. "Find your leak: voltaicmarketing.com/diagnostic" | Conversational wrap. "Something to think about next time you check your analytics." |
| One line. No urgency manipulation. No "don't miss out." | One line. Leaves the insight, not the pitch. |
| Must connect directly to the post's educational content | Feels like a natural ending, not a missing CTA |
| Contractions required | Contractions required |

## Image Text Rules

- Headline on image: 8 words max. Skimmer must get it instantly.
- Subtext on image: 25 words max. Only what can't go in the caption.
- Never use em dashes in image text
- Never use Not-X-But-Y structure in image headlines
- Stats in image text must be bold and visually distinct
- Avoid single-character text glitches by keeping words short and common (no rare compound words)
