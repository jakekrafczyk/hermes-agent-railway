# MobileMD Content Strategy — Cleveland West-Side At-Home Medical Care

Client-specific research for adapting the social-visual-content pipeline to MobileMD.
Use as a template/reference for other local healthcare clients.

## Service Profile
- **Service:** Same-day in-home urgent/primary care for people under 65
- **Pricing:** $150/visit (flat rate, no insurance accepted — positioned as simpler than co-pay roulette)
- **Coverage:** Cleveland's West Side suburbs — Avon, Westlake, Bay Village, Rocky River, Lakewood, Fairview Park, North Olmsted
- **Demographic:** Affluent ($400K–$800K homes), family-oriented, active in Facebook community groups and Nextdoor
- **Source:** [cleveland.com profile](https://www.cleveland.com/community/2026/01/house-calls-make-a-comeback-with-new-mobilemd-service-on-the-westside.html)

## Audience Trust Factors
- Cleveland/Midwest audiences are **highly skeptical** of inauthentic marketing
- "AI-generated" = "corporate" and "not from here" — a death sentence for local healthcare
- 82% of consumers can spot AI content (Column Five Media, 2025)
- 50% can correctly identify AI copy unprompted (Bynder study)
- Consumers **distrust** AI-generated content even when identical to human-written material (Forbes, 2026)

### What Builds Trust
- **Real people** (actual MobileMD staff, not stock photos or AI people)
- **Recognizable locations** (Westlake Crocker Park, Bay Village lakefront, Rocky River bridge, Avon Lake shoreline)
- **Local language** ("the west side," local weather, Cleveland sports references)
- **Community involvement** (local events, sponsorships, partnerships)
- **Video > photos** for authenticity (harder to fake video realistically)

## Top-Performing Content Types for Healthcare Social

| Content Type | Platform | Engagement | Why |
|-------------|----------|-----------|-----|
| Short educational video (30-60s) | Facebook, Instagram Reels | ★★★★★ | Algorithm favors Reels; highest reach |
| Carousel posts (5-10 slides) | Instagram, Facebook | ★★★★☆ | High save rate; educational; shareable |
| Behind-the-scenes photos | Instagram Stories, Facebook | ★★★★☆ | Authenticity; human connection |
| Team/staff spotlights | Instagram, Facebook | ★★★★☆ | Builds trust; local connection |
| Patient testimonials (video) | Facebook | ★★★★★ | Most trusted format for healthcare decisions |
| Infographics | Instagram, Pinterest | ★★★★☆ | High save rate; reference value |
| Live Q&A sessions | Facebook/Instagram Live | ★★★★☆ | Real-time trust building |

Key: 48% of patients rely on word-of-mouth to choose providers. Social media amplifies word-of-mouth rather than replacing it. Content should feel like a neighbor's recommendation, not a corporate ad.
