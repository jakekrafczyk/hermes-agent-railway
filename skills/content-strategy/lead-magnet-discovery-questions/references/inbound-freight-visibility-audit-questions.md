# Inbound Freight Visibility Audit — Discovery Questions (Worked Example)

**Stakeholder:** Nate, owner of T&S Logistics Group
**Lead Magnet:** Inbound Freight Visibility Audit (PDF)
**GTM Context:** Mid-market manufacturers ($10M–$250M), Piedmont Triad NC,
pain points: no visibility into inbound shipments, vendor-routed freight margin leakage

## Question Set

**1. What does "bad" inbound visibility actually look like for your target shippers day-to-day?**
The GTM plan mentions phone calls and spreadsheets — but I'd love specifics.
For example: do they discover a shipment is late only when it doesn't show up at
the dock? Are they manually pulling tracking from carrier portals one-by-one? Any
war stories or patterns you've seen repeatedly that would resonate with them?

**2. If you were to grade a shipper's inbound visibility on a simple scale (say 1 to 5, or Red/Yellow/Green), what are the 3–5 things that separate a "Green" (excellent) from a "Red" (flying blind)?**
For instance: Are they tracking by PO number? Do they get proactive ETA alerts?
Can they see vendor-routed freight at all? This helps me build a scoring rubric
that actually means something to the prospect.

**3. When you talk to a prospect about taking control of vendor-routed freight, what hidden costs tend to hit the hardest?**
I'm thinking things like: supplier freight markups baked into product cost,
last-minute premium/rush freight to cover a missed delivery, excess safety stock
they're carrying because they don't trust arrival dates, production downtime.
Which of these do you see as the biggest dollar leak for your typical prospect?
Any rough percentages or real examples you've quoted in conversations?

**4. How deep do you want this PDF audit to go?**
Should it be a relatively quick self-assessment someone can work through in 10–15
minutes, or a more comprehensive diagnostic they sit with for 30+ minutes — the
kind of document that feels substantial enough to print out and share with their team?

**5. After they get their score and see their hidden cost estimate, what's the ideal next step?**
Is it booking a call with you to walk through the findings? Getting a custom
savings analysis? A direct link to try the HDships portal? The CTA shapes how the
whole audit is framed, so I want to build toward the right offer.

**6. What's the one insight or "aha" moment you want a prospect to walk away with after taking this audit — even if they don't book a call immediately?**
The best lead magnets plant a seed. Is it: "I'm paying 6–8% more than I realized
for vendor-routed freight"? "I have zero visibility into X% of my inbound
shipments"? Something else you find really sticks with people?
