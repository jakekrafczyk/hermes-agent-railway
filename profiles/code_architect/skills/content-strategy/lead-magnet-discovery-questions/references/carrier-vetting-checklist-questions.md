# Carrier Vetting Checklist — Discovery Questions (Worked Example)

**Stakeholder:** Nate, owner of T&S Logistics Group
**Lead Magnet:** Carrier Vetting Checklist (PDF)
**GTM Context:** Mid-market manufacturers ($10M–$250M), Piedmont Triad NC,
positioning as "human-first, tech-enabled freight partner," emphasis on trust
and safety. Competitors criticized for impersonal service. Checklist is meant
to be a practical, downloadable tool emphasizing trust and safety.

## Question Set

**1. Should this checklist cover carriers only, or carriers and brokers?**
The GTM plan says "evaluating new carriers or brokers." If we include broker
vetting, there are extra items to layer in — surety bond/BMC-84 verification,
double-brokering red flags, financial responsibility checks. I want to make sure
I'm scoping it to what your prospects actually need.

**2. What are the 2–3 biggest mistakes you see mid-market shippers make when they pick a carrier on their own?**
For example: relying on a rate confirmation without checking insurance, not
verifying operating authority, skipping references, taking a lowball rate at face
value. Real stories from your experience will make the checklist feel grounded
and credible, not generic.

**3. What red flags do you personally look for that a shipper might not think to check?**
I've seen things like chameleon carriers (frequent name/business changes to hide
violations), MC numbers issued recently with no inspection history, mismatched
phone numbers or email domains between the carrier's insurance and their load
board profile, refusal to share a certificate of insurance directly. Are there
specific signals you've learned to spot from your time in the industry?

**4. How deep do you want this PDF checklist to go?**
Should it be a relatively quick reference someone can run through in 10 minutes
when evaluating a new carrier, or a more comprehensive document they keep on
file — with explanations of each check, links to FMCSA tools (SAFER, Licensing &
Insurance database), and a scorecard page they can fill out per carrier?

**5. The GTM plan emphasizes trust and the "human-first" angle. How much do you want the checklist to set up T&S as the obvious answer — i.e., "a good partner handles this for you" — versus being a purely neutral educational tool?**
I can write it to subtly frame the pain of doing all this yourself vs. having a
single accountable partner, or keep it purely tactical. Where do you want to
land on that spectrum?

**6. After they download the checklist, what's the ideal next step?**
Is it booking a call for a free carrier network review? A freight cost audit?
Something else? The CTA determines how we frame the checklist's value and where
we lead them next.
